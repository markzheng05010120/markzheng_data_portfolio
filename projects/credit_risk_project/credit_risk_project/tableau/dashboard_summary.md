# Credit Risk Dashboard Summary

## Objective
This dashboard visualizes borrower risk patterns and loan default behavior.

The goal is to help lenders identify higher-risk borrower groups and understand the factors related to loan default.

## Dashboard Sections

### KPI Metrics
- Overall default rate
- Average interest rate
- Average loan amount

### Risk Analysis Charts
- Default rate by loan grade
- Default rate by loan burden group
- Default rate by home ownership
- Default rate by loan intent

### Risk Segmentation
The dashboard combines loan grade and loan burden group to provide deeper borrower risk segmentation.

## Key Insights
- Default risk increases significantly from loan grade A to G
- High loan burden borrowers show much higher default rates
- Renters have higher default rates than homeowners
- Debt consolidation and medical loans show elevated risk
- Combining loan grade and loan burden improves borrower segmentation

## Business Value
This dashboard can help lenders:
- identify higher-risk borrowers
- improve borrower segmentation
- support pricing and approval decisions
- monitor portfolio risk patterns
